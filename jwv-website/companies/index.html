Our companies

Three independent operating businesses, each focused on what it does best.

For sales, products, services, or careers at any of the operating companies, please visit them directly. The links below take you to each company's own site.

ARTech Lubricants

A boutique blender of MIL-SPEC silicones, AGMA gear oils, and specialty greases for steel, mining, rail, defense, and aerospace customers.

Michaelson Fluid Power

Hydraulic systems and authorized repair

Custom hydraulic power units, authorized distribution for Parker, Eaton, and Denison, and authorized repair across industrial, marine, and defense customers.

Visit michaelsonfluidpower.com

How the group is organized

Each operating company has its own management, sales force, customers, and brand. The group's role is governance, capital allocation, and a long horizon — and otherwise to stay out of the way.

If you are a customer of one of these companies, you will rarely interact with JWV Solutions directly. The operating company you work with is the one that's accountable to you, and the relationships you've built there are what we are committed to preserving.
