Who we are

A small group of operating partners, holding industrial businesses for the long run.

JWV Solutions is the parent of two independent industrial businesses — Altaris Industrial and ARtech Lubricants. Together, our operating companies serve aerospace, defense, marine, medical, and industrial customers across the United States from facilities in Rhode Island and Connecticut, with over a century of combined operating expertise across specialty packaging, lubricant blending, and hydraulic fluid power.

How we operate

A long horizon. Real operators. Quiet support.

We hold companies for the people inside them — and the customers they serve — not for an exit timeline.

— The JWV Partners

Independence

Each operating company keeps its name, its team, and its way of working. We don't consolidate brands or strip identity.

Long horizon

No fund timeline, no forced exit. We hold companies for decades, which lets the operators make decisions for the long run.

Operator-led

The partners are operators first. Our role is to support what each company already does well — not to direct it from a distance.
