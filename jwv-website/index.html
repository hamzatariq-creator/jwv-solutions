Who we are

A small group of operating partners, holding industrial businesses for the long run.

JVV Solutions is a long-term industrial holding company focused on acquiring and building specialized manufacturing and technical businesses. Through Altaris Industrial, Artech Lubricants, and Michaelson Fluid Power, we serve critical aerospace, defense, marine, medical, and industrial markets across the United States.

Our companies combine more than a century of operating expertise across specialty manufacturing and packaging, engineered lubricants, and hydraulic fluid power systems. We invest with a long-term perspective, preserving the capabilities that make each business successful while providing the resources and leadership to support continued growth.

How we operate

A long horizon. Experienced operators. Quiet support.

We acquire great industrial businesses to build them, not sell them. We preserve what works, invest where we can add value, and give our companies the support and autonomy to grow for the long term.

— The JVV Partners

Independence

Each company retains its name, its team, and the identity that makes it successful. We preserve what works while providing the business and support to help the business grow.

Long horizon

No fund timeline. No predetermined exit. We invest with a long-term perspective, giving our companies the freedom to make decisions that create lasting value.

Operator-led

We are operators first. We work alongside our companies, supporting their leadership with practical experience, resources, and capital — without managing from a distance.

Disciplined growth

We invest where we can make a meaningful difference — expanding capabilities, strengthening operations, developing people, and pursuing opportunities that position our companies for sustainable growth.
