About JWV Solutions

Building industrial businesses for the long term.

JWV Solutions is an industrial holding company that acquires, supports, and grows specialized businesses for the long term. Today, our group includes Altaris Industrial and ARtech Lubricants. Each company maintains its own identity, leadership, customers, and operating discipline, while benefiting from the capital, experience, and resources of the broader JWV group. Our investment horizon is measured in decades, not fund cycles.

What we are

An industrial holding company by design.

JWV owns and supports specialized industrial businesses serving demanding markets across specialty manufacturing and packaging, lubricants, and hydraulic fluid power.

Our role is to provide capital, governance, strategic support, and practical operating experience while allowing the people closest to each business to lead it.

Our Model

Permanent ownership. Independent businesses. Shared strength.

We acquire businesses with the intention of owning them for the long term. We preserve the brands, capabilities, customer relationships, and operating cultures that made them successful while providing resources to support their next stage of growth.

How We Operate

Capital and perspective at the group level. Decisions close to the customer.

Our operating companies maintain responsibility for their customers, people, and day-to-day operations. JWV focuses on capital allocation, strategic priorities, governance, and opportunities where the resources of the broader group can create additional value.

We believe the best operating decisions are generally made by the people closest to the customer and the work.

Principles

What remains constant as the group grows.

  1. 01

    Independent by design

    Each company retains its identity, leadership, customer relationships, and operating discipline. We preserve what works and support what comes next.

  2. 02

    Operator-led

    Decisions stay close to the customer and the operation. JWV provides experience, resources, and perspective without adding unnecessary layers between leadership and the business.

  3. 03

    Long horizon

    No fund timeline and no predetermined exit. We invest with a horizon measured in decades, allowing our companies to make decisions that create lasting value.

  4. 04

    Disciplined growth

    We invest where we can make a meaningful difference — strengthening capabilities, developing people, improving operations, expanding capacity, and pursuing opportunities that position our companies for sustainable growth.
