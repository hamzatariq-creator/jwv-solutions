About JWV Solutions

A small group of operating partners holding industrial businesses for the long run.

JWV Solutions is the parent of three independent operating companies — Altaris Industrial, ARTech Lubricants, and Michaelson Fluid Power. We exist to support each of them, not to consolidate them. The partners are the same across the group, and we run on a horizon measured in decades.

Meet the companies Partnership inquiries
What we are

An industrial holding company — by intent, not by accident.

We are

An industrial holding company with three operating businesses serving customers across packaging, lubricants, and hydraulic fluid power. We own the businesses; we don't run them day-to-day. We support; we don't direct.

We are not
  • A private-equity fund with an exit timeline.
  • A roll-up that consolidates brands and strips identity.
  • A holding company that outsources operating decisions to consultants.
  • Chasing the next quarter's number.
Decisions

Capital, governance, and a stable horizon — then we stay out of the way.

Each operating company has its own management, its own customers, and its own discipline. When we step in, it is to help an operating company invest in a long-horizon project that a quarterly-driven owner would never approve.

Footprint

Rooted in the US Northeast. Reach across the country.

The group's operating footprint is concentrated in Rhode Island, Connecticut, and surrounding markets. Customer reach extends across the United States, with regulated-industry capability in defense, aerospace, marine, and medical adjacencies.
