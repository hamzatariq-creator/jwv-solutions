Partnerships

For business owners thinking about what comes next.

If you have built an industrial business and are beginning to think about succession, JWV may be the right long-term home for what you have built.

We partner with owners who care not only about the transaction, but about what happens afterward — to their company, their employees, their customers, and the reputation they spent years building.

Start a confidential conversation See how we work

We acquire businesses to build them, not prepare them for the next exit.

JWV is an industrial holding company, not a traditional investment fund. We have no predetermined holding period and acquire businesses with the intention of owning them for the long term.

Our starting point is continuity: preserve the identity, people, customer relationships, and capabilities that made the business successful — then invest thoughtfully in what comes next.

Fit

What we look for

Specialized industrial businesses

Companies with established customers, differentiated capabilities, technical know-how, and a reputation built over time.

Enduring customer value

Businesses that solve important problems for their customers and operate in markets where quality, reliability, technical expertise, and service matter.

Owners who care about what comes next

We work particularly well with owners who value continuity for their employees, customers, and company alongside achieving a fair transaction.

Geography where we can add value

Our primary focus is the Northeast United States, particularly Rhode Island, Connecticut, Massachusetts, and surrounding markets. We will consider opportunities outside the region when there is a strong strategic fit.

Continuity

The partners you meet before closing are the partners you work with afterward.

We believe continuity matters. Our starting point is to preserve the team, brand, customer relationships, location, and operating knowledge that made the business successful. We don't arrive with a predetermined integration plan. Changes are made thoughtfully, with the leadership of the operating company, when they strengthen the business for the long term.

Process

How we work with sellers

  1. 01

    Start with a conversation

    No elaborate process is required. We want to understand the business you have built, why you are considering a transition, and what matters to you after closing. If we don't believe we're the right fit, we'll tell you.

  2. 02

    Understand the business

    If there is mutual interest, we work through a focused and confidential diligence process. Our goal is to understand the business thoroughly while minimizing disruption to employees, customers, and day-to-day operations.

  3. 03

    Build the right transaction

    Every situation is different. We work directly with owners to develop a structure that addresses valuation, transition, continuity, and the owner's objectives.

  4. 04

    Stay involved after closing

    There is no handoff to a temporary transition team. The JWV partners involved in the acquisition remain involved as long-term owners and partners to the operating company.

What you can expect from us

Direct access

You deal directly with the people making the decisions.

Discretion

We understand the sensitivity of exploring a sale. Conversations remain confidential and controlled.

A clear answer

We aim to determine fit early rather than putting owners through an unnecessary process.

Flexibility

Every owner and every business is different. We can discuss succession, continued involvement, management succession, and transaction structure around what makes sense for the business.

A long horizon

We make decisions based on what will strengthen a company over years and decades, not what maximizes the next quarter.

Reach out

You don't need a banker, presentation, or formal process to start a conversation. Tell us a little about your business and what you're considering. We'll respond directly and confidentially.
