Partnerships

For founders thinking about what comes next.

If you have built an industrial business and are starting to think about succession, we may be the right home for it. JWV Solutions partners with owners who care about what happens to their company, their team, and their customers after they step back.

Why this page exists

Most acquirers say what they think a seller wants to hear. Then they cut headcount, retire the brand, and run the business for the next exit.

We are not a fund. We do not have a holding period. We hold our companies for as long as they continue to do good work — usually measured in decades, not years. The partners who built the business often stay involved in some capacity if they want to. The brand stays. The team stays. The customer relationships stay.

What we look for

  • Industrial businesses with real customers, real production, and real technical know-how.
  • Niches with depth — specialty packaging, contract manufacturing, lubricants, fluid power, precision machining, regulated-industry adjacencies, and similar.
  • Founders who care about what happens after they step back — not just the headline number on closing day.
  • US Northeast and adjacent geographies, though we have looked at companies further afield when the fit is right.

How we work with sellers

1. A conversation, not a process

The first call is a conversation. No data room, no LOI, no banker. We want to understand what you've built and what you want for it next. If we are not the right fit, we'll say so directly and the call ends there.

2. Diligence at the seller's pace

If we move forward, diligence is shaped to your timeline and your privacy. We do not run public processes or shop the company.

3. Continuity by default

Our default is to keep the team, the brand, the location, and the operating playbook in place. Changes happen only with the operating company's leadership in agreement.

4. After close

The same partners who closed the deal are the partners you continue to work with. There is no transition team that hands you off to a new manager.

What you can expect from us

  • Direct, partner-to-partner conversation. No intermediaries.
  • Discretion. Conversations stay between us until you decide otherwise.
  • A straight answer about fit early in the process, so you can move on if it isn't right.
  • A long horizon. Decisions made with five and ten year arcs in mind, not the next quarter.
