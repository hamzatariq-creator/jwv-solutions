Partnerships

For founders thinking about what comes next.

If you have built an industrial business and are starting to think about succession, we may be the right home for it. JWV Solutions partners with owners who care about what happens to their company, their team, and their customers after they step back.

Start a confidential conversation See how we work

Most acquirers say what they think a seller wants to hear, then cut headcount, retire the brand, and run the business toward the next exit.

We are not that.

JWV is not a fund. We do not have a holding period. We hold our companies for as long as they continue to do good work — usually measured in decades, not years. The brand stays. The team stays. The customer relationships stay.

Fit

What we look for

Industrial businesses

Real customers, real production, real technical know-how. We are not interested in services-light or asset-light plays.

Niches with depth

Specialty packaging, contract manufacturing, lubricants, fluid power, precision machining, and regulated-industry adjacencies.

Founders who care

Owners who think about what happens after they step back — not just the headline number on closing day.

Northeast US & adjacencies

Most often Rhode Island, Connecticut, Massachusetts, and New York. We have looked further afield when the fit is right.

Continuity

The same partners who close the deal stay with you after.

Our default is to keep the team, the brand, the location, and the operating playbook in place. Changes happen only with the operating company's leadership in agreement. There is no transition team that hands you off to a new manager.

Process

How we work with sellers

  1. 01

    A conversation, not a process

    The first call is a conversation. No data room, no LOI, no banker. We want to understand what you've built and what you want for it next. If we are not the right fit, we'll say so directly and the call ends there.

  2. 02

    Diligence at the seller's pace

    If we move forward, diligence is shaped to your timeline and your privacy. We do not run public processes or shop the company.

  3. 03

    Continuity by default

    Our default is to keep the team, the brand, the location, and the operating playbook in place. Changes happen only with the operating company's leadership in agreement.

  4. 04

    After close

    The partners who closed the deal continue to be the partners you work with. No handoff to a transition team.
